# UNM Warns Public Not to Engage with Entities Claiming Responsibility for Cybersecurity Threat at UNM Health System

## At a glance

The University of New Mexico Health Sciences Center, a part of the University of New Mexico Health System, experienced a cybersecurity incident on Monday, October 26, 2026. The incident affected some end-user devices but did not penetrate core systems or servers [[1](#ref-1)]. The University of New Mexico Health System urged people not to engage with entities claiming responsibility for the attack [[1](#ref-1)][[2](#ref-2)]. The FBI is conducting a forensic analysis of the incident as part of an ongoing criminal investigation [[1](#ref-1)][[3](#ref-3)]. The hospital system is still waiting to hear whether patient data was compromised [[1](#ref-1)]. Some UNM Health employees are still being asked not to turn on their computers [[1](#ref-1)]. UNMH is back online to take all levels of trauma [[1](#ref-1)].

## What happened

The cybersecurity incident at the University of New Mexico Health Sciences Center occurred on Monday, October 26, 2026, and affected some end-user devices [[1](#ref-1)]. The university's IT security teams responded quickly to protect core networks, systems, and servers from further damage [[1](#ref-1)]. The incident did not result in a breach of core systems or servers [[1](#ref-1)]. However, the University of New Mexico Health System asked people not to engage with entities claiming responsibility for the attack [[1](#ref-1)][[2](#ref-2)].

## Why it matters

The cybersecurity incident at the University of New Mexico Health System highlights the importance of protecting sensitive data and maintaining operational continuity in the healthcare sector [[1](#ref-1)][[2](#ref-2)]. The incident affected some end-user devices but did not penetrate core systems or servers [[1](#ref-1)]. Nevertheless, the potential impact on the UNM Health System and the public could be significant if patient data was compromised [[1](#ref-1)].

The FBI is conducting a forensic analysis of the incident as part of an ongoing criminal investigation [[1](#ref-1)][[3](#ref-3)]. The hospital system is still waiting to hear whether patient data was compromised [[1](#ref-1)]. In the meantime, some UNM Health employees are still being asked not to turn on their computers [[1](#ref-1)]. UNMH is back online to take all levels of trauma [[1](#ref-1)].
