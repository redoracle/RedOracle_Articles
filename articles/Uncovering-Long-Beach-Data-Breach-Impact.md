## Introduction

The investigation into the data breach affecting the City of Long Beach has raised significant concerns about the security of personal and health information. Levi & Korsinsky, LLP, a renowned law firm specializing in consumer advocacy, has announced an investigation into this breach to determine the impact on individuals associated with the city.

## Key Highlights

- **Incident Background:** The security incident on the City of Long Beach's network systems was discovered on November 14, 2023.
- **Scope of Breach:** Unauthorized access compromised sensitive data, including personal and financial information, medical records, and more.
- **Notification:** The City began notifying affected individuals on March 18, 2025, after identifying those whose data was compromised.
- **Legal Implications:** A notice was filed with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services on April 14, 2025, to address the breach.

## Insights & Analysis

Levi & Korsinsky, LLP is investigating the breach to determine if affected individuals are entitled to compensation. The firm's reputation in consumer advocacy and its contingency basis for payment underscore its commitment to seeking justice for those impacted by data breaches. The potential consequences of such breaches, including identity theft and financial fraud, highlight the critical need for robust cybersecurity measures in place.

## Impact

The breach at the City of Long Beach serves as a stark reminder of the importance of data security for public institutions. It emphasizes the potential risks individuals face when their sensitive information is compromised and the need for transparency and accountability in handling such incidents.

## Conclusion

The investigation into the Long Beach data breach underscores the critical need for robust data security measures to protect individuals' personal and health information. Levi & Korsinsky, LLP's efforts to seek compensation for affected individuals highlight the importance of consumer advocacy in addressing data breaches. Individuals impacted by the breach are encouraged to review their notification letters and explore their options for compensation.
