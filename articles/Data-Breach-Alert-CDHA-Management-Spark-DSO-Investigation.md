# Data Breach Alert CDHA Management & Spark DSO Investigation

![Image](https://storage.googleapis.com/red_articles/Data-Breach-Alert-CDHA-Management-Spark-DSO-Investigation.avif)

## Introduction

Edelson Lechtzin LLP is currently investigating a significant data breach involving CDHA Management, LLC, and Spark DSO, LLC. This breach, detected in September 2024, has raised concerns about the compromise of sensitive customer data. Individuals affected by this breach are advised to closely monitor their personal information to mitigate potential risks of identity theft and fraud.

## Key Highlights

- **Law Firm:** Edelson Lechtzin LLP
- **Companies Involved:** CDHA Management, LLC, Spark DSO, LLC (Chord Specialty Dental Partners)
- **Incident:** Data breach with unauthorized access to customer data
- **Investigation:** Edelson Lechtzin LLP leading the investigation
- **Discovery Date:** September 11, 2024
- **Breach Period:** August 19, 2024 - September 25, 2024
- **Location:** Chord Specialty Dental Partners in Tennessee
- **Data Compromised:** Names, Addresses, Social Security Numbers, Driver's License Numbers, Bank Account Information, Payment Card Information, Dates of Birth, Medical Information, Health Insurance Information
- **Potential Risks:** Identity theft, fraudulent activities
- **Recommended Actions:** Monitor account statements, review credit reports
- **Contact Information:** Edelson Lechtzin LLP, 411 S. State Street, Suite N-300, Newtown, PA 18940, Phone: 844-696-7492 ext. 2, Email: medelson@edelson-law.com, Website: www.edelson-law.com
