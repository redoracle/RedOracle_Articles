## Introduction

USB-C technology has revolutionized connectivity, offering versatile power and data transfer capabilities. In this article, we explore five surprising gadgets that can be connected to your PC's USB-C port to enhance your workspace.

## Key Highlights

- **HOTO Electric Screwdriver Kit**
  - A cordless electric screwdriver ideal for DIY projects.
  - Offers multiple speed settings and includes various bits.
  - Built-in battery for cordless use.

- **Monitor Light Bar**
  - Attaches to your monitor to provide illumination without glare.
  - Reduces eye strain and enhances visibility on the desk.
  - Perfect for low-light environments.

- **Electric Lighter**
  - Rechargeable lighter using electric arcs for safe ignition.
  - Compact design for easy storage and use at your desk.
  - Convenient for lighting candles or similar items.

- **Mouse Jiggler**
  - Prevents your computer from sleeping by simulating mouse movement.
  - Useful for maintaining productivity during downloads or gaming.
  - Helps avoid inactivity penalties in gaming.

- **Inkless Thermal Printer**
  - Compact printer using thermal technology instead of ink.
  - Ideal for quick printing tasks without ink management hassle.
  - Perfect for users needing a space-efficient printing solution.
