- **Introduction of IDXDR:** Softwarfare's IDXDR offers comprehensive identity threat detection and response capabilities.
- **Context of the Launch:** The launch event at the RSA Conference 2025 in San Francisco, California, highlights Softwarfare's commitment to cybersecurity innovation.
- **Significance of Identity Threat Detection:** With identity-based attacks on the rise, IDXDR aims to equip organizations with tools to swiftly detect and respond to such incidents.
