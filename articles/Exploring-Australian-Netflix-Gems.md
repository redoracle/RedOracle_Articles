## Introduction

Netflix has become a household name in Australia, significantly broadening the reach of Australian television shows to global audiences. The platform offers a diverse array of Australian content, from gritty crime dramas to light-hearted comedies, catering to various tastes and preferences.

## Historical Context

Australian television has a rich history dating back to 1929, evolving significantly over the decades. The industry has produced numerous series and films that have garnered cult followings worldwide.

## Australian Shows on Netflix

Netflix features a variety of Australian shows that showcase the country's storytelling prowess. These series often reflect societal issues while providing engaging narratives.

### Key Shows

#### Stateless
- **Genre:** Drama
- **First Episode:** 1 March 2020
- **Setting:** Port Augusta, South Australia
- **Summary:** Based on true events, 'Stateless' follows four individuals from diverse backgrounds in an asylum-seeker detention center, highlighting the struggles of those seeking a better life.
- **Cast:** Yvonne Strahovski, Asher Keddie, Fayssal Bazzi, Jai Courtney, Marta Dusseldorp, Dominic West, Cate Blanchett

#### Wanted
- **Genre:** Crime Drama
- **First Episode:** 2016
- **Setting:** Queensland and South Australia
- **Summary:** This series follows two strangers who become embroiled in a murder case after witnessing a car crash, leading them on a thrilling escape from both criminals and law enforcement.
- **Cast:** Rebecca Gibney, Geraldine Hakewill, Kerry Fox

#### Tidelands
- **Genre:** Crime Drama
- **First Episode:** 14 December 2018
- **Setting:** Southeast Queensland
- **Summary:** A young woman returns to her hometown and uncovers a hidden commune of outcasts while investigating a conspiracy involving drugs and murder.
- **Cast:** Charlotte Best, Elsa Pataky, Aaron Jakubenko, Marco Pigossi

#### Secret City
- **Genre:** Political Thriller
- **First Episode:** 2016
- **Setting:** Canberra
- **Summary:** This political thriller follows journalist Harriet Dunkley as she uncovers conspiracies and scandals that threaten national security, reflecting real-world tensions between Australia, China, and the USA.
- **Cast:** Anna Torv, Jackie Weaver, Alex Dimitriades

#### Pine Gap
- **Genre:** Political Thriller
- **First Episode:** 14 October 2018
- **Setting:** Alice Springs, Northern Territory
- **Summary:** Set in a joint intelligence facility, this series explores the collaboration between American and Australian analysts amidst geopolitical tensions.
- **Cast:** Parker Sawyers, Tess Haubrich, Jacqueline McKenzie

#### Glitch
- **Genre:** Drama
- **First Episode:** 9 July 2015
- **Setting:** Castlemaine, Victoria
- **Summary:** In a small town, seven people mysteriously return from the dead, leading to a series of supernatural events that intertwine their fates.
- **Cast:** Patrick Brammall, Genevieve O’Reilly, Emma Booth

#### The Beautiful Lie
- **Genre:** Drama
- **First Episode:** 18 October 2015
- **Setting:** Melbourne, Kyneton, Daylesford
- **Summary:** A modern retelling of Tolstoy's 'Anna Karenina', this series explores themes of love, fidelity, and personal choices in the life of a sporting star.
- **Cast:** Alice Bell, Jonathan Gavin, Blake Ayshford

#### Deep Water
- **Genre:** Crime Drama
- **First Episode:** 5 October 2016
- **Setting:** Bondi, Sydney
- **Summary:** This gritty crime drama follows detectives investigating a series of suspicious deaths linked to the gay community, drawing attention to societal issues.
- **Cast:** Yael Stone, Noah Taylor, Jeremy Lindsay Taylor

#### Jack Irish
- **Genre:** Detective Crime Series
- **First Episode:** February 11, 2016
- **Setting:** Fitzroy, Melbourne
- **Summary:** Following a former criminal lawyer turned private investigator, this series captures the essence of Melbourne's vibrant culture.
- **Cast:** Guy Pearce

#### Janet King
- **Genre:** Drama
- **First Episode:** 27 February 2014
- **Setting:** Sydney
- **Summary:** A spin-off from 'Crownies', this series follows a tough prosecutor navigating high-profile cases and personal challenges.
- **Cast:** Marta Dusseldorp, Vince Colosimo, John Howard

#### Sisters
- **Genre:** Comedy
- **First Episode:** 25 October 2017
- **Setting:** Melbourne
- **Summary:** A humorous exploration of family dynamics as a woman discovers she has over 100 siblings due to her father's career as a fertility doctor.
- **Cast:** Lucy Durack, Antonia Prebble, Maria Angelico

#### Offspring
- **Genre:** Comedy
- **First Episode:** August 15, 2010
- **Setting:** Melbourne
- **Summary:** This series follows Nina Proudman, an obstetrician navigating her chaotic personal life, blending humor with poignant moments.
- **Cast:** Asher Keddie, Kat Stewart, Matthew Le Nevez

#### The Letdown
- **Genre:** Comedy
- **First Episode:** 4 May 2016
- **Setting:** Sydney and Adelaide
- **Summary:** A comedic take on the challenges of motherhood, following a new mom as she navigates the ups and downs of parenting.
- **Cast:** Alison Bell, Sarah Scheller, Duncan Fellows

#### Rake
- **Genre:** Comedy
- **First Episode:** 2010
- **Setting:** Sydney
- **Summary:** Focusing on a self-destructive barrister, this series combines humor with the complexities of legal and personal challenges.
- **Cast:** Richard Roxburgh, Matt Day, Adrienne Pickering

#### Operation Buffalo
- **Genre:** Comedy
- **First Episode:** 31 May 2020
- **Setting:** New South Wales and South Australia
- **Summary:** Inspired by true events, this series humorously depicts the challenges faced during British nuclear testing in Australia.
- **Cast:** Ewen Leslie, James Cromwell, Jessica de Gouw

#### The Bureau of Magical Things
- **Genre:** Fantasy
- **First Episode:** 8 July 2018
- **Setting:** Brisbane and the Gold Coast
- **Summary:** A teenager discovers a magical realm where she possesses powers that can unite various magical species.
- **Cast:** Kimie Tsukakoshi, Julian Cullen, Mia Milnes

## Conclusion

The diverse range of Australian shows available on Netflix not only entertains but also reflects the unique cultural narratives and societal issues of Australia. With a blend of genres and themes, these series offer something for everyone, making them a worthy alternative to Hollywood productions. 

### Key Insights

- Australian television continues to evolve, gaining recognition for its originality and storytelling.
- The global reach of platforms like Netflix allows Australian content to find audiences far beyond its borders.
