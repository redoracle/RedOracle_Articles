## Introduction

In response to escalating cybersecurity threats, DEF CON hackers have launched the Franklin Project to secure US water systems. This initiative, led by cybersecurity experts and volunteers, aims to protect critical infrastructure by addressing vulnerabilities in water utilities across the country.

## Key Highlights

- **Who:** DEF CON volunteers, including cybersecurity experts and enthusiasts
- **Organizers:** Jake Braun, co-founder of DEF CON Franklin, and various partner organizations
- **What:** Franklin Project initiative to provide free cybersecurity services to US water utilities
- **When:** Launched in August 2024, with rapid expansion as of August 2025
- **Where:** Initial locations in Indiana, Oregon, Utah, and Vermont
- **Why:** Addressing increased cyber threats from state-sponsored actors targeting critical infrastructure
- **How:** Deploying volunteers for cybersecurity assessments, training on best practices, and implementing security measures

## Insights & Analysis

The cybersecurity landscape for US water systems has become increasingly precarious, with the Franklin Project aiming to enhance resilience. By focusing on small utilities lacking cybersecurity measures, the initiative educates about real threats and provides essential support.

The project's success stories include preventing phishing attacks and improving cybersecurity practices. With partnerships and support from various organizations, the Franklin Project aims to create a sustainable model for ongoing cybersecurity in the water sector.

## Impact

The Franklin Project signifies a crucial step in fortifying the cybersecurity of US water systems, addressing vulnerabilities in national infrastructure. As the initiative expands, it sets a precedent for community-driven cybersecurity efforts in other sectors, emphasizing the importance of proactive protection.

## Conclusion

The Franklin Project's proactive approach to safeguarding water systems highlights the significance of volunteer-driven cybersecurity initiatives. By leveraging expertise and partnerships, this project not only enhances the security of critical infrastructure but also fosters a culture of cybersecurity awareness and resilience in communities.
