## Introduction

In a significant development within the cybersecurity sector, CloudSEK, a Bengaluru-based cybersecurity startup, has successfully raised $19 million in its Series A2 and B1 funding rounds. This funding milestone reflects the growing investor confidence in CloudSEK's proactive approach to threat intelligence, AI models, and market expansion.

## Key Highlights

- **Stakeholders:** CloudSEK, MassMutual Ventures, Inflexor Ventures, Prana Ventures, Tenacity Ventures, Commvault, Meeran Family, StartupXSeed, Neon Fund, Exfinity Ventures
- **Funding Amount:** $19 million
- **Previous Funding:** $7 million in Series A round
- **Time Gap:** 3.5 years since last funding
- **Date:** May 20, 2025
- **Company Background:** Founded in 2015 by Rahul Sasi
- **Focus:** Predictive threat intelligence
- **Services:** Detecting Initial Attack Vectors (IAVs) such as leaked credentials and exposed APIs
- **Market Position:** Over 60% of net new revenue from international markets
- **Fastest Growing Market:** United States
- **Enterprise Clients:** More than 250 enterprises across sectors like banking, healthcare, and technology
- **ARR Growth:** Tripled in the past 24 months
- **Future Plans:** Extending Series B1 into a B2 tranche to attract new investors and enable partial exits for early backers

## Insights & Analysis

### Funding Details

New investors in CloudSEK include MassMutual Ventures, Inflexor Ventures, Prana Ventures, Tenacity Ventures, and Commvault, while existing investors like Meeran Family, StartupXSeed, Neon Fund, and Exfinity Ventures continue to support the company. This funding will be utilized to enhance AI models, integrate platforms, and expand operations globally, with a focus on the rapidly growing US market.

### Company Background

Founded in 2015 by Rahul Sasi, CloudSEK specializes in predictive threat intelligence, allowing it to identify potential threats before they materialize. The company's emphasis on early detection of threats, such as leaked credentials and exposed APIs, sets it apart from traditional cybersecurity approaches.

### Market Performance

CloudSEK has reported significant revenue growth, with over 60% of its net new revenue now coming from international markets. Serving a diverse client base of more than 250 enterprises, the company has tripled its Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) in the past 24 months, showcasing its rapid expansion and increasing demand for its services.

### Future Strategies

CloudSEK's plan to extend its Series B1 funding round into a B2 tranche demonstrates its proactive approach to scaling operations and strengthening investor relations. By attracting new investors and providing opportunities for early backers to partially exit, the company aims to fuel further growth and innovation in the cybersecurity space.

## Impact

The successful funding round not only bolsters CloudSEK's financial position but also underscores its commitment to advancing cybersecurity solutions. With a focus on innovation and market expansion, particularly in the US, CloudSEK is well-positioned to meet the increasing demand for proactive cybersecurity measures.

## Conclusion

CloudSEK's $19 million funding achievement marks a significant milestone in the company's journey towards proactive cyber solutions. By leveraging advanced threat intelligence, AI models, and strategic market expansion, CloudSEK is poised to lead the cybersecurity industry with innovative approaches and robust investor support.
