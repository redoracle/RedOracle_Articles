## Introduction

In the realm of cybersecurity, the threat of voice phishing, also known as vishing, has become increasingly prevalent. To combat this rising danger, Arsen, a cybersecurity startup based in Paris, France, has unveiled an innovative AI-powered Vishing Simulation module. This module aims to equip organizations with the necessary tools to train employees effectively against voice phishing attacks, leveraging cutting-edge AI technology to enhance cybersecurity readiness.

## Key Highlights

- **Company:** Arsen, a cybersecurity startup.
- **Date:** Announced on June 13, 2025.
- **Location:** Paris, France.
- **Objective:** Address the growing threat of voice phishing attacks.
- **Technology:** Utilizes AI-generated voices and adaptive dialogue systems for realistic simulations.

## Insights & Analysis

### Vishing Simulation Module Features

- **Dynamic Adaptation:** The AI system adjusts to employee responses for personalized training.
- **Realistic Dialogue:** Simulations include unscripted dialogue to mimic real-life scenarios.
- **Multilingual Support:** Capable of producing voices in various languages and accents.

### Stakeholders

- **Company:** Arsen.
- **CEO:** Thomas Le Coz.
- **Target Audience:** CISOs, compliance officers, risk managers, employees at all levels.

### Implications

- **Organizational Resilience:** Benchmark resilience against voice phishing threats.
- **Employee Training:** Enhance reflexes and awareness to combat manipulative voice-based attacks.

## Impact

The introduction of Arsen's Vishing Simulation module signifies a significant advancement in cybersecurity training. By harnessing AI technology to create realistic and scalable training scenarios, Arsen aims to bolster organizations' security posture against the evolving landscape of social engineering threats, particularly voice phishing.

## Conclusion

Arsen's AI-powered Vishing Simulation module represents a pivotal step in preparing organizations for the challenges posed by voice phishing attacks. By focusing on comprehensive and realistic training, Arsen strives to enhance cybersecurity readiness across all levels of an organization.
