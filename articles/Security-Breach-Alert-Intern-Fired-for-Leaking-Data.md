# Security Breach Alert Intern Fired for Leaking Data

![Image](https://storage.googleapis.com/red_articles/Security-Breach-Alert-Intern-Fired-for-Leaking-Data.png)

## Introduction

The recent incident involving Edward Coristine, a 19-year-old intern at Path Network, has brought to light critical issues surrounding government efficiency, national security risks, vetting procedures, and the handling of sensitive information. Coristine's termination for leaking internal data has sparked concerns about the security practices in place within government-related technology initiatives.

## Key Highlights

- **Background**: Coristine was part of Elon Musk's DOGE initiative, tasked with analyzing government data.
- **Firing**: Path Network confirmed his termination due to leaking proprietary information, emphasizing a zero-tolerance policy.
- **Access Rights**: Despite being fired, Coristine retained access to company systems, raising concerns about potential misuse.
- **Political Ramifications**: Senator Ron Wyden expressed worries about the national security risks posed by allowing unvetted individuals access to sensitive information.
- **Online Behavior**: Coristine's history of engaging in discussions about hacking and his controversial online presence have raised doubts about his suitability for roles requiring security clearance.
- **Government Response**: Path Network and government agencies are pushing for stricter controls over access to sensitive data following the incident.

## Insights & Analysis

The case of Edward Coristine highlights the urgent need for comprehensive vetting strategies within government contract roles. It underscores the delicate balance between innovative approaches to government efficiency and the protection of national security. The incident serves as a critical reminder of the importance of rigorous vetting procedures and the potential impact of individual actions on national security.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, the dismissal of Edward Coristine sheds light on the vulnerabilities in security practices and the handling of sensitive information within government-related technology initiatives. It emphasizes the necessity for enhanced vetting processes to prevent similar breaches in the future. As organizations navigate the intersection of government efficiency and national security, ensuring robust security measures and thorough vetting procedures is paramount.
