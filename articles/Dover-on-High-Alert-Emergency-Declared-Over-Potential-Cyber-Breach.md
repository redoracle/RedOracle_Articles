# Dover on High Alert Emergency Declared Over Potential Cyber Breach

![Image](https://storage.googleapis.com/red_articles/Dover-on-High-Alert-Emergency-Declared-Over-Potential-Cyber-Breach.avif)

## Introduction

Dover Mayor Robin Christiansen declared a state of emergency on January 29, 2025, in response to a potential cyber security breach. The city is on high alert as officials work to protect residents' personal information and address the cyber threat.

## Declaration of Emergency

- Mayor Christiansen declared a state of emergency on January 29, 2025.
- The emergency is effective from 8 a.m. on January 29, 2025, due to an imminent cyber security threat.

## Nature of the Cyber Breach

- Specific details about the cyber breach remain unclear.
- Confidential information may have been exposed online, prompting immediate actions to safeguard data.

## Emergency Powers Granted

- The emergency declaration grants the City Manager authority to protect citizens.
- Permitted actions include public work performance, entering contracts, employing temporary workers, and other necessary regulations.

## Government Response

- Dover officials are collaborating with cyber security experts to stabilize the situation.
- Ongoing assessments are being conducted, with updates expected as the situation develops.

## Ongoing Situation Development

WBOC will continue to provide updates on the situation as more information becomes available.

## Conclusion

Dover is facing a critical cyber security breach, leading to the declaration of a state of emergency. The city is taking proactive measures to address the threat and protect residents' personal information. Stay informed with WBOC for the latest updates on this developing story.
