## Introduction

**Colabor Group secures forbearance with lenders and Investissement Québec through October 15, 2025**. This development provides temporary covenant relief for Colabor as the company addresses near term liquidity and operational impacts stemming from a cybersecurity incident earlier in 2025. The forbearance gives Colabor space to pursue amendments to its debt facilities and to work toward longer term stability while maintaining normal operations across Quebec and Atlantic Canada. Keywords integrated: colabor, forbearance, lenders, investissement-quebec, debt facilities, covenants, liquidity, amendments, quebec, atlantic-canada, credit facility, ar facility, cyber incident, 2025.

## Overview and Context

**Who**

- Colabor Group Inc. TSX GCL in cooperation with The Toronto Dominion Bank, Bank of Montreal and The Bank of Nova Scotia and with Investissement Québec under their respective credit facilities.

**What**

- Forbearance agreements under Colabors amended and restated senior first ranking secured credit facility A&R Credit Facility and under Investissement Québecks subordinated and highly subordinated credit facilities IQ Credit Facilities. The agreements temporarily suspend lenders rights and remedies related to anticipated covenant defaults for the third and fourth quarters of 2025.

**When**

- Forbearance effective as of September 5, 2025 and continuing through October 15, 2025.

**Where**

- Corporate communications issued from Saint Bruno de Montarville, Quebec with operations across Quebec and Atlantic Canada.

**Why**

- The forbearance provides flexibility to stabilize liquidity and pursue amendments after a cybersecurity incident reported in July 2025 while supporting Colabors long term stability and growth objectives.

**How**

- Through negotiated forbearance agreements reached following discussions disclosed in June 2025 and ongoing amendment talks designed to strengthen balance sheet and liquidity.

## Key Provisions and Scope

Colabors forbearance agreements allow temporary avoidance of enforcement actions by the lenders and by Investissement Québec in respect of anticipated covenant breaches. The agreements cover specified financial covenants applicable to the third and fourth quarters of 2025 and are conditioned on continued compliance with defined financial and operational undertakings during the forbearance window.

- Scope of relief
  - Temporary suspension of rights and remedies tied to anticipated covenant defaults for the third and fourth quarters of 2025.
- Parties to the agreements
  - Colabor; principal lenders including The Toronto Dominion Bank, Bank of Montreal and The Bank of Nova Scotia; Investissement Québec.
- Outlook within scope
  - Forbearance supports ongoing discussions about potential long term amendments to the A&R Credit Facility and to the IQ Credit Facilities.

## Strategic and Financial Implications

The forbearance period provides immediate operational stability and short term liquidity breathing room while Colabor negotiates potential permanent amendments to its debt facilities. This relief is particularly material in the wake of the July 2025 cyber incident which intensified the need for a measured financial pathway forward.

- Near term benefits
  - Liquidity and stability that reduce immediate default risk while negotiations continue.
- Potential long term outcomes
  - Amendments could align debt terms with Colabors longer term business plan and ensure continuity of supply to hotel restaurant institutional HRI and retail channels.
- Creditor hierarchy considerations
  - IQ facilities are subordinated to the A&R Facility which shapes recovery dynamics and negotiation leverage across creditors.

## Company Background and Operations

Colabor serves as a distributor and wholesaler of food and related products primarily to HRI customers in Quebec and Atlantic Canada and to the retail market. The companys product mix includes specialty fish and seafood, meat and broadline food items delivered across its network.

- Markets served
  - Quebec and Atlantic Canada; hotels restaurants institutions and retail consumers.
- Product focus
  - Fish and seafood, meat and other food related products through broadline operations.

## Timeline, Next Steps and Outlook

Colabor and its lenders will use the forbearance period as a window to continue negotiations on potential amendments intended to improve resilience and liquidity. The company commits to operating in the ordinary course meeting obligations to customers and suppliers and advancing its business plan while talks proceed.

- Timeline events
  - July 2025: Colabor reports a cybersecurity incident affecting operations.
  - June 2025: Colabor disclosed engagement in discussions with lenders and Investissement Québec regarding debt facilities.
  - September 5, 2025: Announcement of forbearance agreements with principal lenders and Investissement Québec.
  - October 15, 2025: Forbearance period expiration subject to ongoing compliance and progress on amendments.
- Immediate focus
  - Secure amendments that support long term stability and growth while preserving day to day operations.
- Monitoring and milestones
  - Management board lenders and stakeholders will evaluate progress and provide updates as discussions progress.
