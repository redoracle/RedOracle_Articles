## Introduction

The Tea app, designed for women to share experiences about potential online matches, faced a significant data breach that compromised user safety. This incident sheds light on the risks associated with online gossip, whisper networks, and the delicate balance between anonymity and privacy in digital platforms.

## Key Highlights
- The Tea app, marketed as 'the safest place to spill tea,' allows users to share experiences about potential online matches.
- A data breach on July 25, 2025, exposed sensitive user information, including government IDs and direct messages.
- Online trolls exploited the breach to threaten the safety of women using the app.
- The breach led to class action lawsuits against Tea, highlighting the legal consequences of such incidents.
- The concept of 'whisper networks' has gained traction, especially in the context of women's safety.
- Digital whisper networks can lead to harmful consequences, including mob behavior and defamation.
