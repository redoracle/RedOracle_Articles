## Introduction

Vietnam is experiencing a significant tech revolution, attracting tech enthusiasts, investors, and entrepreneurs alike. Initiatives such as the Hacker Residency in Da Nang and the introduction of a 10-year golden visa are driving this transformation. This report explores the implications of these developments on Vietnam's economy and the tech community.

## Key Highlights

- **Hacker Residency in Da Nang:** In November 2025, a month-long Hacker Residency will take place in Da Nang, Vietnam, hosting experienced developers aiming to advance their startups or indie projects.
- **Golden Visa Program:** Vietnam announced a new 10-year golden visa program on May 8, 2025, targeting long-term residents, investors, and professionals to boost the economy and tourism sector.
- **Electronics Markets in Ho Chi Minh City:** Ho Chi Minh City serves as a central hub for sourcing electronics and hardware, offering a wide range of components and tools for tech enthusiasts and professionals.
- **Cultural and Economic Implications:** Vietnam's initiatives aim to attract talent and investment, positioning the country as a competitive player in the global tech landscape.

## Insights & Analysis

### Hacker Residency in Da Nang, Vietnam

- **Organizers' Vision:** The Hacker Residency organizers envision cultural exchange and innovation opportunities by hosting developers in Vietnam.
- **Supportive Community:** The residency aims to create a supportive environment for developers to collaborate and innovate.
- **Visa Policies:** Vietnam's favorable visa policies enhance the residency's appeal, attracting tech talent to Southeast Asia.

### Vietnam's 10-Year Golden Visa

- **Tourism Goals:** Vietnam aims to attract millions of international visitors in 2025 through the golden visa program, simplifying access for tourists.
- **Attracting High-Net-Worth Individuals:** The golden visa program enhances Vietnam's appeal as a destination for high-net-worth individuals, fostering economic growth.
- **Cultural Evolution:** Vietnam's cultural and economic landscape is evolving to accommodate foreign residents and investors.

### Hacker Abroad: Vietnam's Electronics and Hardware Markets

- **Thriving Markets:** Ho Chi Minh City's electronics markets cater to diverse needs, from DIY projects to industrial applications.
- **Local Culture:** The blend of commerce and culture in the markets showcases Vietnam's appeal to tech enthusiasts and travelers.
- **Street-Side Stalls:** Informal vendors play a significant role in the electronics supply chain, offering convenience and local charm.

### Impact

Vietnam's initiatives, including the Hacker Residency and the golden visa program, signify a strategic move to enhance its position in the global tech ecosystem. By attracting talent and investment, Vietnam aims to foster innovation, economic growth, and job creation, making it an attractive destination for tech enthusiasts and entrepreneurs.

## Conclusion

Vietnam's tech revolution, driven by initiatives like the Hacker Residency and the golden visa program, showcases the country's commitment to innovation and economic development. The vibrant electronics markets in Ho Chi Minh City further highlight Vietnam's growing capabilities in technology and manufacturing. As Vietnam continues to attract foreign investment and talent, it is poised to become a significant player in the global tech landscape.
