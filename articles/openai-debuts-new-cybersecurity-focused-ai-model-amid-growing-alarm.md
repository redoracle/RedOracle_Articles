# OpenAI Debuts New Cybersecurity-Focused AI Model Amid Growing Alarm about AI-Driven Cyber Attacks

## At a glance

OpenAI, a leading artificial intelligence (AI) research laboratory, has recently debuted a new cybersecurity-focused AI model called GPT-6 Cyber [[1](#ref-1)]. This advanced model is designed specifically for investigating complex cybersecurity issues and identifying weaknesses in software systems [[1](#ref-1)][[3](#ref-3)].

## What happened

OpenAI's new AI model, GPT-6 Cyber, has already reportedly uncovered "thousands" of exploits [[2](#ref-2)]. The model is being distributed through Daybreak Red, OpenAI's restricted access program for advanced cybersecurity work [[1](#ref-1)]. GPT-6 Cyber's capabilities include discovering previously unknown vulnerabilities, developing methods to test them, and assisting security teams in patching the identified weaknesses [[3](#ref-3)].

## Why it matters

OpenAI's new AI cybersecurity model plays a crucial role in finding major software flaws, helping security teams identify and address weaknesses in their systems [[3](#ref-3)]. This not only enhances the overall cybersecurity landscape but also ensures the protection of critical infrastructure and organizations' data [[1](#ref-1)][[3](#ref-3)].

## Technical details

During simulations, GPT-6 Cyber successfully identified two previously unknown vulnerabilities in engineering software used for managing protective relay data in a power plant facility [[3](#ref-3)]. These flaws could potentially expose protection settings and circuit-breaker trip logic [[3](#ref-3)].

## What defenders should do

While no concrete actionable steps for the audience have been provided in the sources, it is recommended for organizations and security researchers to go through OpenAI's approval process and meet its requirements to gain access to GPT-6 Cyber [[1](#ref-1)][[3](#ref-3)].
