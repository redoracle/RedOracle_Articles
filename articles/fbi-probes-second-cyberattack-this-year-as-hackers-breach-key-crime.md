## At a glance
The FBI's National Crime Information Center (NCIC) database, a critical tool for law enforcement nationwide, was targeted in a cyberattack. This intrusion follows a September attack by the ShinyHunters hacking group on an FBI jobs website. Additionally, the US Department of Agriculture (USDA) reported a cyberattack on its payment system for farmers, raising concerns about potential access to sensitive biological and research data.

## What happened
Hackers breached the FBI's National Crime Information Center (NCIC), according to three sources familiar with the matter. The FBI is investigating the hack to determine the unauthorized entry point, but the extent of compromised information remains unclear. The NCIC database is essential for federal, state, and local authorities, aiding in tracking fugitives, monitoring registered sex offenders, checking for outstanding warrants, and conducting gun background checks.

The USDA informed employees that its payment system for farmers was targeted in a cyberattack, as reported by NBC, citing two former USDA officials. The hackers may use this system as a backdoor to access more sensitive material, the officials warned. The USDA handles critical information related to food safety, agriculture, and environmental issues, making any unauthorized access potentially severe for national security, public health, and the economy.

## Why it matters
The potential consequences of these cyberattacks extend beyond personal data theft. The NCIC database plays a vital role in law enforcement operations, and any inaccuracies or compromises could directly impact officer and public safety. A former FBI official emphasized the importance of the system's accuracy, stating that officers rely on it daily to identify wanted fugitives and check for outstanding warrants.

The breach of the USDA's payment system raises significant concerns about the security of sensitive biological and research data. Unauthorized access to this information could have far-reaching implications, including threats to national security, public health, and economic stability.
