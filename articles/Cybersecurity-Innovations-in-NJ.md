# Cybersecurity Innovations in NJ

![Image](https://storage.googleapis.com/red_articles/Cybersecurity-Innovations-in-NJ.avif)

## Introduction

The cybersecurity landscape in New Jersey is experiencing rapid evolution, marked by significant partnerships and advancements within local companies. This article sheds light on key developments, particularly highlighting collaborations with the global cybersecurity leader CrowdStrike, and acknowledges achievements in the industry. As cyber threats grow more sophisticated, the demand for robust cybersecurity solutions becomes increasingly crucial for businesses across various sectors.

## Key Developments

### Cognizant
- **Who:** Cognizant, a Teaneck-based technology company.
- **What:** Announced a strategic partnership with CrowdStrike to enhance enterprise security transformation.
- **When:** January 2025.
- **Where:** Teaneck, New Jersey.
- **Why:** To streamline security operations and improve threat mitigation using CrowdStrike's AI-native Falcon platform.
- **How:** By leveraging Falcon products, Cognizant aims to consolidate fragmented legacy systems and strengthen cybersecurity postures.
- **Quote:** CEO Ravi Kumar S: "Cognizant is committed to staying at the forefront of cybersecurity innovation."

### Commvault
- **Who:** Commvault, a data protection solutions provider based in Tinton Falls.
- **What:** Entered into a partnership with CrowdStrike to enhance cyber resilience and data protection.
- **When:** January 2025.
- **Where:** Tinton Falls, New Jersey.
- **Why:** To deliver layered threat detection and fast data recovery for joint customers.
- **How:** By integrating CrowdStrike's real-time threat intelligence with Commvault's cloud-first cyber resilience capabilities.
- **Quote:** CEO Alan Atkinson: "We are committed to empowering businesses with the tools they need to safeguard their data."

### Gaming Laboratories International (GLI)
- **Who:** Gaming Laboratories International, based in Lakewood.
- **What:** Received the Services Provider of the Year award at the Global Gaming Awards EMEA Edition 2025.
- **When:** January 2025.
- **Where:** ICE Barcelona, Spain.
- **Why:** To recognize excellence in supporting the gaming sector, including cybersecurity.
- **How:** Awarded by an independent panel of industry leaders.
- **Quote:** CEO James Maida: "We are thrilled and humbled that the industry recognized us for our industrious efforts."

### BCN
- **Who:** BCN, a Morristown-based company.
- **What:** Formed a strategic partnership with Adaptiv Networks to enhance SD-WAN solutions.
- **When:** January 2025.
- **Where:** Morristown, New Jersey.
- **Why:** To provide advanced site-to-site connectivity and cloud-based security.
- **How:** By combining BCN's managed internet connectivity with Adaptiv's SD-WAN overlay expertise.
- **Quote:** President Julian Jacquez: "Our collaboration underscores our dedication to offering comprehensive connectivity management solutions."

## Conclusion

The cybersecurity industry in New Jersey is witnessing significant advancements through strategic partnerships and innovative solutions. Companies like Cognizant, Commvault, GLI, and BCN are at the forefront of enhancing security measures and addressing the evolving challenges posed by cyber threats. As the demand for cybersecurity solutions continues to grow, these collaborations will play a crucial role in shaping a more secure digital landscape for businesses in New Jersey and beyond.
