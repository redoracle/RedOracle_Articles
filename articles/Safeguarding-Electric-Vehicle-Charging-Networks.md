## Introduction

As the electric vehicle (EV) infrastructure expands, the importance of cybersecurity in protecting EV charging networks becomes paramount. This report delves into the key cybersecurity standards, vulnerabilities, and implications for the future of EV charging stations.

## Key Highlights

- **Cybersecurity Standards**: IEC 62443 and ISO 15118 are crucial for securing EV charging stations.
- **Vulnerabilities**: Account hijacking, impeded charging, and backdoor access are significant risks.
- **Implications**: The security of charging networks is vital for the stability of the power grid and the EV ecosystem.

## Insights & Analysis

### Key Cybersecurity Standards

- **IEC 62443**: Focuses on securing industrial automation and control systems, with specific requirements for EV charging station components.
- **ISO 15118**: Ensures secure communication between electric vehicles and charging stations, supporting advanced functionalities like Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) and Smart Charging.
- **ISO 27001**: Manages information security within charging networks, emphasizing data protection and sensitive information management.
- **OCPP 2.0.1**: Facilitates communication between charging stations and management systems, ensuring secure operations.

### Emerging Security Threats

- Despite standards, vulnerabilities like account hijacking and hardware vulnerabilities persist, posing risks to users and the power grid.
- Consequences of hacks include theft of electricity, manipulation of charging stations, and account takeovers due to API authorization issues.

### Impact

The lack of security assurance in the EV charger market underscores the need for enhanced API security and better hardware choices. Manufacturers must prioritize user security to prevent fraud and ensure reliable charging infrastructure.

## Conclusion

The intersection of cybersecurity and EV infrastructure is crucial. Stakeholders, including Charge Point Operators and EV charger manufacturers, must collaborate to enhance security measures and provide a safe charging experience for all users.
