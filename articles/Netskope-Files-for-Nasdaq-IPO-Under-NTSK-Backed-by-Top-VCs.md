## Executive Overview

- **Who** Netskope, a California based cloud security company founded in 2012, backed by Lightspeed Venture Partners, Iconiq, Accel, Sequoia, and Sapphire Ventures.
- **What** The company filed to go public by submitting an SEC regulatory filing to pursue an IPO on Nasdaq under the ticker NTSK.
- **When** Filing occurred on August 22, 2025, aligning with a broader wave of tech ipo activity expected post Labor Day 2025.
- **Where** Nasdaq will be the listing venue while Netskope continues to operate globally from its California headquarters.
- **Why** To access public market capital to accelerate growth in cloud security where revenue expansion is rewarded even as profitability remains a concern for many 2025 tech offerings.
- **How** Through a standard IPO process anchored by an S 1 filing and regulatory review, disclosing financials cash position and investor composition to prospective public investors.
