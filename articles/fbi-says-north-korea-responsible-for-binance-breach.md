# FBI Identifies North Korea as Responsible Party for $6.3 Billion Binance Breach

## At a glance

The FBI has attributed the Binance breach, which resulted in unauthorized withdrawals and an estimated financial impact of approximately $6.3 billion, to North Korea [[1](#ref-1)][[2](#ref-2)][[3](#ref-3)]. This attribution comes from the identified responsible group, TraderTraitor. North Korea, however, has denied these allegations, calling them 'absurd rhetoric' [[4](#ref-4)][[5](#ref-5)].

## What happened

On October 29, 2026, the cryptocurrency exchange, Binance, reported a significant security breach, leading to unauthorized withdrawals [[1](#ref-1)]. The breach is estimated to have resulted in the theft of approximately $6.3 billion in digital assets [[1](#ref-1)][[2](#ref-2)][[3](#ref-3)]. Preliminary investigation reports suggest that the attackers gained access to the exchange through a compromised laptop belonging to a Binance DevOps engineer [[1](#ref-1)]. Although the exact points of breach have not been confirmed, subsequent investigations uncovered additional vulnerabilities [[1](#ref-1)].

## Why it matters

The attribution of the Binance breach to North Korea is significant because of the financial impact of the breach and the involvement of a nation-state actor. This highlights the growing risks of state-sponsored cyberattacks targeting the cryptocurrency industry [[1](#ref-1)][[3](#ref-3)].
