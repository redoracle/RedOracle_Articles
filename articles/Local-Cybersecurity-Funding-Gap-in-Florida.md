## Introduction

The budget negotiations in Florida have brought to light a significant funding gap in local cybersecurity assistance grants. While the Florida House proposes a $15 million allocation for these grants, the Senate has not offered any funding, indicating a pressing need for increased cybersecurity measures at the local level.

## Key Highlights
- The House proposes a $15 million allocation for local cybersecurity assistance grants, while the Senate has not provided any funding.
- The budget conference in Tallahassee emphasized the necessity for comprehensive cybersecurity measures across local governments.
- The proposed funding would be administered through the Department of Management Services (DMS), with a focus on enhancing grant activities.
- A previous $40 million grant program aimed at improving cybersecurity measures was implemented during the 2024-25 fiscal year.

## Insights & Analysis
The disparity in funding proposals between the House and Senate underscores the importance of addressing cybersecurity vulnerabilities at the local level. The lack of Senate funding highlights potential gaps in cybersecurity preparedness that need urgent attention. By allocating resources to local cybersecurity assistance grants, Florida can enhance its overall cybersecurity posture and better protect critical infrastructure and sensitive data.

## Impact
The proposed funding allocation, if approved, could significantly bolster cybersecurity defenses at the local level, ensuring that municipalities and government entities are better equipped to mitigate cyber threats. By investing in cybersecurity measures, Florida can proactively address emerging cyber risks and safeguard against potential cyberattacks that could compromise sensitive information and disrupt essential services.

## Conclusion

The funding gap in local cybersecurity assistance grants in Florida presents a critical challenge that requires immediate action. By bridging this gap and allocating resources to enhance cybersecurity measures, Florida can strengthen its resilience against cyber threats and safeguard its digital infrastructure. It is imperative for policymakers to prioritize cybersecurity funding to protect the state's critical assets and ensure the security and privacy of its residents.
