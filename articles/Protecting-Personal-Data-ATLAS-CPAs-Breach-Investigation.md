# Protecting Personal Data ATLAS CPAs Breach Investigation

![Image](https://storage.googleapis.com/red_articles/Protecting-Personal-Data-ATLAS-CPAs-Breach-Investigation.avif)

## Introduction

The investigation into the ATLAS CPAs & Advisors PLLC data breach by Levi & Korsinsky, LLP has raised concerns about the security of personal information and the potential risks of identity theft and financial fraud. This article delves into the details of the breach, the investigation process, and the implications for affected individuals.

## Key Highlights

- **Data Breach Discovery:** ATLAS CPAs & Advisors PLLC detected unauthorized activity on its systems in December 10, 2024.
- **Investigation Initiation:** Third-party cybersecurity experts were engaged to assess the breach's nature and scope in December 2024.
- **Notification to Authorities:** ATLAS filed a notice with the Attorney General of Massachusetts on March 25, 2025, and began notifying affected individuals.
- **Compromised Information:** Names, Social Security numbers, and financial information were potentially accessed.
- **Legal Investigation:** Levi & Korsinsky, LLP is investigating the breach to determine compensation eligibility for affected individuals.

## Insights & Analysis

The breach at ATLAS CPAs & Advisors PLLC underscores the importance of robust data security measures to prevent identity theft and financial fraud. Companies handling sensitive personal information must prioritize cybersecurity to protect individuals from potential harm. Levi & Korsinsky, LLP's involvement highlights the legal implications for companies failing to safeguard personal data and the rights of affected individuals to seek compensation.

### Impact

The breach at ATLAS CPAs & Advisors PLLC serves as a critical reminder of the significance of cybersecurity in safeguarding personal data.
