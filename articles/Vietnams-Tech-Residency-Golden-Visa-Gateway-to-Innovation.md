## Introduction

Vietnam is emerging as a significant hub for technology and investment, with two notable initiatives: a Hacker Residency program in Da Nang and a new 10-year golden visa aimed at attracting foreign investors and professionals. These initiatives reflect Vietnam's growing appeal as a destination for tech entrepreneurs and high-net-worth individuals.

## New Hacker Residency in Da Nang, Vietnam

### Overview
- The Hacker Residency in Da Nang is an innovative initiative aimed at fostering a collaborative environment for developers.
- Organized by Tony Dinh and Minh-Phuc Tran, the program seeks to replicate the success of similar programs in tech hubs like San Francisco.

### Program Structure
- Duration: One month
- Participants: 10 experienced developers
- Cost: Free for residents, with sponsorship opportunities available

### Community Feedback
Initial reactions from the community express excitement about the potential for cross-cultural collaboration and productivity.

## Vietnam's 10-Year Golden Visa Program

### Overview
- The Vietnamese government is targeting high-net-worth foreigners and professionals with a new 10-year golden visa program.
- The initiative aims to attract long-term residents and investors to bolster Vietnam's economy and tourism sector.

### Visa Categories
- Investor Visa: 10 years with a pathway to permanent residency after 5 years
- Talent Visa: 5 years for skilled professionals with easy renewal
- Golden Visa: 5-10 years with the possibility of extension

### Economic Impact
Vietnam saw a 23.8% increase in foreign visitors in the first quarter of 2025, indicating a growing interest in the country.

## Hacker Abroad: Vietnam’s Electronics and Hardware Markets

### Insights
- Ho Chi Minh City serves as a central hub for sourcing electronics and hardware.
- Markets are well-stocked with components like resistors, segment displays, and LED strips, catering to hobbyists and professionals alike.

### Key Features
- Diverse range of electronic components available
- Street-side stalls offer quick access to parts and tools
- Presence of resident hackers and repair services indicate a thriving DIY culture

## Cultural and Economic Context

### Vietnam's Appeal
- Vietnam is positioning itself as an affordable and culturally rich destination, with major cities like Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi offering quality services and international schools.
- The local cuisine, infrastructure development, and tourism growth contribute to Vietnam's attractiveness.

### Tourism Statistics
In the first quarter of 2025, Vietnam welcomed over 7.67 million foreign visitors, a 23.8% increase year-on-year.

## Conclusion

The Hacker Residency and golden visa initiatives signify Vietnam's strategic move to become a global player in technology and investment. By attracting skilled professionals and investors, Vietnam aims to enhance its economic landscape and cultural diversity. As these programs unfold, they may set a precedent for similar initiatives in Southeast Asia, potentially transforming the region into a vibrant tech hub.
