## Protection Mitigation and Recommendations

• **Governance and planning**  
 • Maintain an incident response plan with defined roles and tabletop exercises.  
 • Verify insurance coverage for cyber theft fraud and business interruption.

• **Access and authentication**  
 • Enforce multi factor authentication for administrative and financial accounts.  
 • Apply least privilege for staff access to payment and accounting systems.

• **Payment security and POS hygiene**  
 • Use PCI DSS compliant processors and end to end encryption for card data.  
 • Keep POS firmware and payment terminal software current and conduct quarterly vulnerability scans.

• **Network and monitoring**  
 • Segment POS and reservation systems from general staff networks.  
 • Implement centralized logging and real time transaction anomaly detection.

• **Vendor risk**  
 • Assess third party vendors with access to payments or guest data and require security attestations and incident notification clauses.

• **Resilience**  
 • Maintain immutable offline backups and test restore procedures.  
 • Engage reputable digital forensics firms when losses are material.
